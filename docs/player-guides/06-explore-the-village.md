# Explore the village: The Last Light

Open `/explore` or choose **Walk into the shadows** on the homepage. This is
a short atmospheric prologue to the AI council. You explore on foot with a
lantern, find up to six optional keepsakes, and reach the council fire. You can
continue beyond the fire into the forest before taking your seat.

## Find your way

1. Choose **Enter the village**. Sound starts only after you interact.
2. Walk toward the lantern on your left. When its **Examine** prompt appears,
   press **E** or select the prompt to recover its seal and read the note.
3. Follow the lane north to the old well. Examine it for the second seal.
4. Continue toward the chapel and recover the third keepsake near its door.
5. Approach the empty chair south of the fire beyond the chapel. **Sit down &
   choose players** opens the AI game setup. Or continue north to explore.

Seals can be collected in any order. The compass shows your heading, the
objective explains nearby routes, and **Map & journal** shows your position,
trails and every recovered note. A werewolf encounter interrupts the lane when
you approach while facing the watchman; no keepsake is required. Outside that
encounter, explore at your pace.

## Beyond the lane

The lane is the quickest route, but the village is walled by a palisade and
open to explore. Side streets lead to:

- **The market square** (east of the well): the tavern, the smithy's forge,
  stalls, a pillory, and a lamplighter making his rounds.
- **The old graveyard** (west, behind the chapel): rows of headstones, a
  mausoleum, an open grave and someone kneeling beside it.
- **The Hollow farm** (south-west): a barn, haystacks, a paddock and a scarecrow.
- **The mill pond** (north-east of the council): a turning waterwheel and a jetty.
- **The hunter's hut** (north-west, at the forest edge).

## The Blackwood forest

Continue north past the council, through the gap in the palisade. The playable
area is more than three times its previous size. Open **Map & journal** with
**J** to see the trails and your current position (the pale arrow).

- **Moon temple:** take the western fork. Enter between the columns to find a
  broken moon seal. Candlelight, tangled roots and distant tolling surround
  the ruined sanctuary. Its central aisle is walkable.
- **Abandoned settlement:** take the northeastern fork. Two roofless houses
  contain broken rafters and boarded windows. Read the diary in the lane
  between them; wood creaks and a faint breath come from the empty rooms.
- **Whispering falls:** follow the trail east past the houses. Animated water
  falls from a rocky cliff into a misty pool. The water gets louder as you
  approach. Examine the charm on the bank; the pool and cliff are impassable.

All three discoveries are optional. Follow the lantern trail south to the
council; you do not need to restart or finish the journal to begin the AI game.

Villagers still awake turn to watch you. When **Speak with …** appears, press
**E** to hear what they saw. They add atmosphere only; nothing they say is
required, and none of them are council roles.

Sound is positional; headphones help. The fire, forge, mill and creature come
from where they stand, and footsteps change on cobbles, mud, grass and wood.
Distant lightning is followed by thunder. Reduced motion turns lightning, rain,
grain and camera sway off.

## The watchman’s hunger

Approach the two figures near the old well, facing the
watchman. He doubles over, transforms, and attacks the other villager. The
creature feeds beside the fallen lantern before turning toward you. This is
an animated, stylized horror scene with a fallen body and a dark blood pool.

Keep the creature in front of you and your lantern **lit**. Within nine metres,
the protection meter fills; holding the light on it for about three seconds
drives it away. Turn with the arrow keys, drag, or the touch turning controls.
You can also retreat out of its range or run north toward the chapel. On a
keyboard, hold **Shift** while moving to outrun it. The hunt lasts at most
sixteen seconds before it retreats.

If it reaches you without the lantern facing it, the night pauses on a retry
screen. **Return to the lane** places you before the encounter with your
lantern lit and all collected seals intact. After surviving, your journal gains
a witness account for this visit. Reloading replays the encounter; its state
and witness account are not saved. The watchman and victim are anonymous
prologue characters, not assigned roles or deaths in the live AI council.

## Controls

### Your traveller and camera

First-person is the default. **Choose your traveller** on the intro or pause
screen offers four free animated presets: the woodsman, huntress, wanderer and
wayfarer. Selecting one enables a third-person follow camera. Press **V** or
use the camera button to switch views at any time while playing. The camera
pulls closer near solid obstacles and temporarily hides the avatar when there
is not enough room. Preset choice lasts for this visit; no photo is uploaded
and no external avatar service is used. Photo-generated likenesses are deferred.

Run and jump to cross low fences, crates, carts and broken walls. You can land
on a low obstacle and jump again, or step off it. You cannot double-jump, jump
through buildings or trees, or cross the water and outer palisade. Pause also
freezes an in-progress jump. The selected traveller is only your exploration
appearance; it does not select your council seat or secret role.

| Action | Keyboard | Pointer / touch |
|---|---|---|
| Walk | W A S D; Up / Down | Click nearby ground; hold movement arrows |
| Turn | Left / Right | Drag across the scene; hold turning arrows |
| Run | Hold Shift while walking | Toggle Run, then move |
| Jump | Space | Select Jump |
| First / third person | V | Select the camera-view button |
| Examine | E | Select the contextual prompt |
| Lantern | F | Select the lantern control |
| Map & journal | J | Select Map & journal |
| Pause | Escape | Select Pause |

Click-to-walk follows a straight line and stops at obstacles. Walk around a
building rather than clicking through it. **Use captured mouse** in Pause
enables continuous mouse look on browsers that support it; Escape releases
capture and pauses. Drag-to-look is always available.

## Comfort and saving

Pause stops movement, the encounter and waterfall animation, and mutes the
soundscape. Ambient fire and sky effects can continue. Opening the journal
or a clue also freezes gameplay while you read. Switching
away from the page pauses automatically, so a held key cannot leave you moving.

The pause menu has brightness, sound, and reduced-motion controls. Reduced
motion starts from your system preference and removes head bob, lantern sway,
rain, moving fog, light variation, and the brief visual apparition. Its story
cue remains readable. Reduced motion also removes transformation tremor, the
creature's pounce height, feeding bob, and running gait, while preserving readable character
poses and the encounter's rules. Player jumping still changes height so low
barriers can be crossed; avatar gait is frozen. Pause and journal freeze the
encounter clock, including the protection meter and capture checks. The soundscape combines
locally served CC0 recordings with a synthesized waterfall. No microphone is
used. Roars change direction
and loudness with the creature’s location. Pausing freezes audio playback as
well as the encounter; Sound off silences it.

Recovered seals are stored in local browser storage. **Continue the night**
starts at the village entrance with those discoveries intact; position and
settings are not saved. If storage is blocked, progress lasts for the current
visit. **Start again** asks before clearing only the prologue's discoveries.

The 3D scene requires WebGL. If it cannot start, the fallback offers a reload
and a direct link to the AI council.

## How this connects to the live game

The prologue is an authored environmental story, separate from live agents.
The figures at its fire are the established cast's artwork. No agents are
running yet, and no secret roles have been assigned. Its clues are atmosphere,
not evidence about a later game's randomly assigned roles.

After **Take your seat**, choose a seat and model configuration as usual.
The existing night, discussion, voting, observability, and debrief workflows
continue from there.
