# Explore the village: The Last Light

Open `/explore` or choose **Walk into the shadows** on the homepage. This is
a short atmospheric prologue to the AI council. You explore on foot with a
lantern, collect three seals, and reach the fire at the north end of the lane.

## Find your way

1. Choose **Enter the village**. Sound starts only after you interact.
2. Walk toward the lantern on your left. When **Examine the lantern** appears,
   press **E** or select the prompt to recover its seal and read the note.
3. Follow the lane north to the old well. Examine it for the second seal.
4. Continue toward the chapel and recover the last seal near its door.
5. Approach the fire beyond the chapel. **Enter the council clearing** finishes
   the prologue, and **Take your seat** opens the live AI game setup.

Seals can be collected in any order. The compass shows your heading, the
objective points to your next missing discovery, and the journal preserves
every recovered note. A werewolf encounter interrupts the lane after your first
seal. Outside that encounter, explore at your pace.

## The watchman’s hunger

Approach the two figures near the old well with at least one seal, facing the
watchman. He doubles over, transforms, and attacks the other villager. The
creature feeds beside the fallen lantern before turning toward you. This is
an animated, stylized horror scene with a fallen body and a dark blood pool.

Keep the creature in front of you and your lantern **lit**. Within nine metres,
the protection meter fills; holding the light on it for about three seconds
drives it away. Turn with the arrow keys, drag, or the touch turning controls.
You can also retreat out of its range or run north toward the chapel. On a
keyboard, hold **Shift** while moving to outrun it. The hunt lasts at most
sixteen seconds before it retreats.

If it reaches you without the lantern facing it, the night pauses on a retry
screen. **Return to the lane** places you before the encounter with your
lantern lit and all collected seals intact. After surviving, your journal gains
a witness account for this visit. Reloading replays the encounter; its state
and witness account are not saved. The watchman and victim are anonymous
prologue characters, not assigned roles or deaths in the live AI council.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Pointer / touch |
|---|---|---|
| Walk | W A S D; Up / Down | Click nearby ground; tap movement arrows |
| Turn | Left / Right | Drag across the scene; tap turning arrows |
| Hurry | Hold Shift while walking | — |
| Examine | E | Select the contextual prompt |
| Lantern | F | Select the lantern control |
| Journal | J | Select Open journal |
| Pause | Escape | Select Pause |

Click-to-walk follows a straight line and stops at obstacles. Walk around a
building rather than clicking through it. **Use captured mouse** in Pause
enables continuous mouse look on browsers that support it; Escape releases
capture and pauses. Drag-to-look is always available.

## Comfort and saving

Pause stops movement and the atmosphere's animation and mutes the soundscape.
Opening the journal or a clue also stops the world while you read. Switching
away from the page pauses automatically, so a held key cannot leave you moving.

The pause menu has brightness, sound, and reduced-motion controls. Reduced
motion starts from your system preference and removes head bob, lantern sway,
rain, moving fog, light variation, and the brief visual apparition. Its story
cue remains readable. Reduced motion also removes transformation tremor, jump
height, feeding bob, and running gait, while preserving readable character
poses and the encounter's rules. Pause and journal freeze the encounter clock,
including the protection meter and capture checks. The audio is synthesized
locally and uses no microphone.

Recovered seals are stored in local browser storage. **Continue the night**
starts at the village entrance with those discoveries intact; position and
settings are not saved. If storage is blocked, progress lasts for the current
visit. **Start again** asks before clearing only the prologue's discoveries.

The 3D scene requires WebGL. If it cannot start, the fallback offers a reload
and a direct link to the AI council.

## How this connects to the live game

The prologue is an authored environmental story, separate from live agents.
The figures at its fire are the established cast's artwork. No agents are
running yet, and no secret roles have been assigned. Its clues are atmosphere,
not evidence about a later game's randomly assigned roles.

After **Take your seat**, choose a seat and model configuration as usual.
The existing night, discussion, voting, observability, and debrief workflows
continue from there.
